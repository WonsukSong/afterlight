# Afterlight

**Engineering insight. Business judgment.**

AI news for understanding the mechanism behind a change and what it means for customers, economics, competition and execution. The primary reader is its creator, Wonsuk Song, an engineer developing business judgment.

- Daily: concise cards, substantive mechanism explanations, evidence and specific business implications.
- Weekly: ranked shifts, cross-story connections and decisions worth investigating.
- Papers: prior limitations, new mechanisms, selective results, useful sourced visuals and product implications.

The November 11 editorial review covers all 43 daily stories across 22 editions, three weekly reviews and four papers. Stable links, original publication dates and signed Editor’s Takes are preserved.

This repository contains the public GitHub Pages distribution. The Afterlight service supplies published content; the hourly Actions workflow validates the feed, commits changed content and deploys Pages. No writer credentials or editor drafts are included.
